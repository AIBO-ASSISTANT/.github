# Code of Conduct

The AIBO project expects direct, respectful, and professional collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat contributors with respect, especially during disagreement.
- Critique code, decisions, and tradeoffs rather than people.
- Share context early when a decision affects other repositories.
- Give actionable review feedback.
- Accept review feedback as part of the engineering process.
- Avoid exclusionary, insulting, harassing, or hostile behavior.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern.
- Intentionally bypassing agreed security or review processes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block merges, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately with enough context to be reviewed by maintainers.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers through the least public channel available to the team. Do not use public issues for sensitive interpersonal or security concerns.
